LifeSync 2.0

A personal productivity and finance management companion app enhanced with an intelligent local AI assistant. Build on a Manual-First, AI-Assisted philosophy.

Quick Features Guide

  • mic Local AI Assistant: Dictate expenses, routines, and goals.
  • database Offline-First: Transactions occur in client IndexedDB.
  • sound_detection_dog_barking Speech Synthesis: Flat-voice vocal feedback & synth sounds.
  • settings_suggest Trust Model: All calculations are verified, not AI-assumed.
